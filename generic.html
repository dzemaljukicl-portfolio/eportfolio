How this site is organised

Evidence with context

This portfolio is structured by module, with each page covering the learning focus, selected activities, reflection and supporting materials.

The activity summaries provide context; they are not substitutes for the original assignments. Entries marked “To attach” identify files or links to add after checking the final coursework. The purpose is to make my learning process visible without presenting unuploaded artefacts as already available.
