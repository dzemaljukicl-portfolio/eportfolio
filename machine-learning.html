01 / Introduction

Module overview

This module explores the full machine-learning workflow: framing a question, preparing data, selecting methods, validating results and interpreting outcomes. I am particularly interested in what makes models reliable and useful outside a controlled experiment.

Key areas of learning

  • ✦ Data preparation
  • ✦ Model evaluation
  • ✦ Cross-validation
  • ✦ Ethics & explainability
  • ✦ Business insights
