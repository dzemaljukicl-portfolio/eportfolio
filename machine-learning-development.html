Professional development

Skills developed, gaps identified by tutor feedback, and concrete actions for future ML projects.

This is a reflective, qualitative skills matrix informed by the coursework and tutor feedback—not a formal externally assessed competency rating. Actions are proposed next steps, not completed achievements.

DEVELOPED

Technical ML implementation

Evidence: Model programming and evaluation in Airbnb analysis; HOG-SVM/CNN comparison.

Action: Keep comparing sensible baselines and making experiment configurations reproducible.

DEVELOPING

Quantitative evaluation

Evidence: Cross-validation, MAE/RMSE, macro F1, ROC-AUC and robustness analysis.

Action: Document evaluation design, uncertainty, class disparities, and out-of-distribution errors.

PRIORITY

Critical analysis and academic writing

Evidence: Both tutor feedback reports requested deeper critical interpretation, alternatives and deployment implications.

Action: Before drafting, allocate words to trade-offs; keep a decision/assumption log; use professor feedback as a review checklist.

DEVELOPED

Virtual teamwork and communication

Evidence: Six meetings, Kanban board, rotated facilitation, PR review and report-section ownership.

Action: Include explicit assumption-challenge points in future team meetings.

DEVELOPING

Research and ethical awareness

Evidence: AI-writing peer discussion, NIST risk framework, XAI and Fashion-MNIST limitations.

Action: Assess stakeholder harms, data representativeness, privacy and practical human review before deployment.

DEVELOPING

Commercial awareness

Evidence: Airbnb business framing, price uncertainty and segment recommendations.

Action: Define how business gains, errors and implementation risks would be measured after launch.

Practical action plan

  • For my next ML report: reserve space for limitations, alternative approaches, technical uncertainty and real-world implications before filling in methodology.
  • During each experiment: record feature decisions, model alternatives, validation rationale and one potential failure case.
  • Before a model recommendation: review class-level errors, dataset representation, operational constraints and human oversight.
  • For the next group project: add a recurring agenda item for challenging modelling assumptions, not only tracking work.
  • Before submission: re-read previous tutor feedback and confirm that conclusions have evidence and counterarguments.
